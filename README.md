## 🧠 Operators in Python

Operators are special symbols or keywords used to perform operations on values and variables.

### 1. Bitwise Operators

Bitwise operators work on numbers at the binary level.

Common bitwise operators:

```text
&   AND
|   OR
^   XOR
~   NOT
<<  Left Shift
>>  Right Shift
